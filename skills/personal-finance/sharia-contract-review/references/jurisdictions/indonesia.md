# Indonesia — jurisdiction reference

`as_of: 2026-10-20` · Re-verify anything marked volatile before relying on it.

## Who governs what

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **DSN-MUI** | Issues fatwas on sharia matters; not a regulator |
| **OJK** | Regulator: banking, capital market, sharia instruments (POJK) |
| **BEI / IDX** | Exchange; publishes ISSI/JII index constituents |
| **BAZNAS** | National zakat body; sets annual nisab and rates |
| **LPS** | Deposit insurance; covers sharia deposits too |
| **Kemenag** | Bridges MUI and government on religious rulings |

## Equity screening

Two layers that are often confused:

1. **Fatwa DSN-MUI No. 80/2011** — business activity must not contradict sharia;
   interest-based debt / total assets ≤ 45%; interest income / total revenue ≤ 10%.
2. **Operative DES criteria** — POJK 8/2025 replaced POJK 35/2017:
   * non-halal income (interest plus other) / total revenue **≤ 5%**, in force
     from 25 April 2026;
   * interest-based debt / total assets **45% → 33%**, phased over up to 10
     years, schedule set by OJK;
   * OJK may set different ratios, and KPKS has recommended adjusting the DES
     debt ratio.
3. **Daftar Efek Syariah (DES)** — OJK decision, reviewed around **May and
   November**. ISSI is the index of all DES-listed shares on IDX.

**Rule for this pack:** cite the fatwa and the operative POJK/DES criterion
separately. Do not report "45%" as the current cap without checking the phase.
If the phase is unclear, say so and cap the verdict at `questionable`.

## Indices

ISSI (all DES shares), JII, JII70, IDXSHAGROW, IDXMESBUMN. Constituent counts
change at each DES review and at incidental additions after IPOs — always pull
the latest BEI announcement rather than quoting a number from memory.

## Fixed income and sukuk

* **SBSN** — government sukuk (retail series: SR, ST; institutional: PBS, SBSN).
  Fatwa basis includes DSN-MUI 69, 70, 71, 72, 76/2008 covering ijarah sale and
  lease back and asset-to-be-leased structures.
* **Corporate sukuk** — check structure, asset backing, and guarantee per
  `sharia-sukuk-analysis`.
* Bank Indonesia instruments: SRBI and sukuk-based liquidity tools; check the
  current instrument's underlying before treating it as sharia-compliant.

## Banking and products

* **POJK 4/2026** (investment products in sharia banking; in force 29 April
  2026): separates savings from investment products, requires suitability
  assessment, separate fund management and accounting, disclosure, and consumer
  protection. Existing products have up to 2 years to conform.
* **Spin-offs**: POJK 11/2023 and UU 4/2023 require sharia business units to
  spin off or transfer portfolios on a schedule; several are in progress.
* **Rahn**: four rahn fatwas were consolidated in the DSN-MUI 61st plenary
  (September 2026); confirm the current fatwa number before citing.
* **Gold**: Fatwa 163/2025 (sharia gold ETF), Fatwa 166/2026 (bullion
  business). Digital gold is under active fatwa development — check status.

## Crypto

* **Fatwa 140/DSN-MUI/XI/2021** and the 2021 Ijtima Ulama: crypto **as
  currency is haram** (gharar, dharar, contrary to law); as a commodity it is
  **not validly tradable unless** it meets sil'ah requirements — physical
  existence, known quantity, ownership, deliverability, and a clear underlying
  benefit.
* OJK stated in February 2026 that sharia classification is still under
  discussion with DSN-MUI; no blanket halal ruling exists.
* Government position reaffirmed in October 2026: the 2021 Ijtima remains the
  reference.
* **Practical default for Indonesia: non-compliant**, with a narrow exception
  only where the asset demonstrably meets sil'ah. Do not import Malaysian or
  Gulf permissiveness.

## Zakat

**BAZNAS SK No. 15/2026** (21 February 2026), nisab for income and services:

* 85 g gold (14 karat), valued at the **2025 average gold price**
* **Rp 91,681,728 / year** or **Rp 7,640,144 / month**
* rate 2.5%, on gross income and services, paid when received

Gold zakat: nisab 85 g (BAZNAS uses Antam buyback price). Silver: 595 g. The
nisab in rupiah is reset annually — always check the current SK.

## Practical implications for an Indonesian sharia investor

* Screen against **DES/ISSI**, not a generic global Islamic index.
* Prefer liquid ISSI names; small-cap ISSI constituents can be hard to exit in
  a stressed market.
* SBSN and sharia money market funds are the natural cash and fixed-income
  sleeve; check the underlying and the current yield.
* Gold (including sharia gold ETF and bullion per Fatwa 166/2026) is a common
  hedge, but check the structure and storage.
* Crypto: default excluded under Fatwa 140/2021.
* Re-check DES status after each May/November review and after corporate
  actions, since a name can leave the universe without the business changing.
