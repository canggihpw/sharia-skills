# Contract taxonomy

Reference sheet for identifying and auditing an akad. Skills should name the
contract type before analysing it, because the compliance test differs per type.

## Exchange and financing

| Contract | Shape | Core condition | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Murabaha** | Sale at cost plus disclosed markup, often deferred | Seller owns and possesses the goods before selling to the buyer | Bank pays supplier directly and books a "loan with markup"; no real ownership or risk |
| **Salam** | Prepaid purchase of a specified fungible good delivered later | Subject matter precisely specified; quantity, quality, date certain | Delivery risk shifted back to buyer via side agreement |
| **Istisna** | Commission to manufacture/build with staged payment | Specification agreed; price fixed; penalty clauses must not be riba | Manufacturer guarantees a financial return unrelated to work performed |
| **Ijarah** | Lease of an asset or usufruct for rent | Lessor owns the asset and bears ownership risk (major maintenance, insurance, destruction) | Lessee bears structural maintenance; rent charged on a loan |
| **Ijarah muntahia bittamlik** | Lease ending in transfer of ownership | Separate transfer mechanism (gift or sale) after the lease term | Ownership transferred at signing; effectively a disguised loan |

## Partnership and agency

| Contract | Shape | Core condition | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mudarabah** | Capital from one party, work from another; profit shared by ratio | Loss borne by capital provider unless manager is negligent; ratio fixed ex ante | Capital guaranteed; manager paid a fixed return |
| **Musharakah** | Joint capital contribution; profit by agreement, loss by capital ratio | Loss strictly proportional to contribution | One partner guaranteed a return; capital guaranteed |
| **Musharakah mutanaqisah** | Diminishing partnership, one party buys out the other | Buyout at market value or agreed formula, not a guaranteed return | Fixed buyback at face value + fixed profit = loan |
| **Wakalah** | Agency for a fee | Agent acts within mandate; fee is for service | Agent guarantees the outcome |
| **Wakalah bil istithmar** | Investment agency | Profit ratio agreed; loss on capital unless misconduct | Capital guarantee |

## Security, guarantee, and risk-sharing

| Contract | Shape | Core condition | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rahn** | Pledge of an asset as security | Pledgor retains ownership; pledgee holds possession only | Pledgee takes benefit from the pledged asset |
| **Kafalah** | Guarantee of an obligation | Guarantor may charge a service fee, not a fee proportional to the guaranteed amount | Fee scales with the debt |
| **Takaful** | Cooperative risk pooling via tabarru' fund | Contributions are donations; surplus distribution per policy | Operator guarantees returns; fund invested in conventional instruments |
| **Qard hasan** | Benevolent loan | No increase for deferral | Any fee tied to the amount |

## Structures to treat with care

- **Tawarruq / commodity murabaha** — permissible with strict conditions in some
  standards, prohibited as organised tawarruq in others. Always flag.
- **Bay' al-'inah** — sale and repurchase to generate cash; prohibited by the
  majority, permitted by some Shafi'i jurisprudence and Malaysian practice.
- **Bay' al-dayn** — sale of debt at a discount; generally prohibited, which is
  why sukuk need tangible assets to be tradable.
- **Hiyal** — legal stratagems that reach a prohibited outcome through form.
  Substance over form: if the economic result is a loan, treat it as a loan.

## The audit question

For any product, ask in order:

1. What is the underlying asset or service?
2. Who owns it, and when?
3. Who bears which risk, and when?
4. What exactly is the return, and what is it attached to?
5. Where does any penalty or late fee go?
6. Do the documents match the described structure?
